RecheckLab v0.2: one bounded LLM recheck

Published Qwen3-4B SEVRA outcomes; secondary replay analysis, no new LLM inference.

Predict HELP (wrong to correct), HURT (correct to wrong), or NO_CHANGE (correctness unchanged). A separate policy chooses whether to keep the initial answer or use the recorded revision. Skipping never certifies correctness.

1,999 source maths questions; 1,221 target questions. 19 initial-only features. All rollouts stay with their question.

Frozen gate target accuracy: 76.67%; completion rule: 76.67%; keep initial: 76.13%. Target outcome macro-F1: 0.394 (majority baseline 0.323); multiclass log loss: 0.322. No tested label budget improved mean accuracy over the frozen gate. The learned gate does not beat the completion rule in accuracy.

Model diagnostics, safeguards and limitations
  • One solver checkpoint, one transfer direction, one partition seed.
  • Inherited public correctness labels; answer keys are absent and were not independently rescored.
  • Threshold-only adaptation is not probability calibration or LLM fine-tuning.
  • Adaptation subsets overlap; their SD measures subset variability, not independent-experiment uncertainty.
  • Former public evaluation data are explicitly repartitioned for a new protocol; these are not the original SEVRA benchmark scores.
  • Rare HURT source labels and strong source/target class and feature shift limit transfer.
  • Published model/action metadata checks cannot establish unreleased prompt and sampling compatibility.
  • Skip recheck keeps the initial answer and does not certify correctness.
