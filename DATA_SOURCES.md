# Data provenance and attribution

## Included public outcome logs

Dataset: Sajib-006/sevra-recovery-outcomes

URL: https://huggingface.co/datasets/Sajib-006/sevra-recovery-outcomes

Pinned revision: 10411137fcaa02b3bd549a2285b43ffe315417f6

Authors: Sajib Acharjee Dip, Dawei Zhou, and Liqing Zhang. Related paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19808

The original card is included unchanged in data/raw/DATASET_CARD.md. Raw Parquet files contain benchmark questions, generated Qwen3-4B responses, outcome annotations, and token accounting. Generated responses and annotations are described as research/evaluation data. Underlying MATH, GSM8K, and CommonsenseQA terms remain applicable. No new license is assigned to these included data by this project.

The pilot processes only active_verify records from the source train and target commonsenseqa files. math500 and gsm8k are included unchanged for later evaluation; they are not used in the first pilot.

## Optional own-collection benchmarks

GSM8K: https://huggingface.co/datasets/openai/gsm8k — MIT, as identified in its dataset card. Original authors: Cobbe et al. (2021), Training Verifiers to Solve Math Word Problems.

BoolQ: https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/boolq — CC BY-SA 3.0, as identified in its dataset card. Original authors: Clark et al. (2019), BoolQ: Exploring the Surprising Difficulty of Natural Yes/No Questions. License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

Downloaded benchmark question files are for the user's research workflow and retain those terms. prepare_benchmarks.py records source dataset and split for each example. Answer keys are withheld from inference prompts.

## Generated project artifacts

The source predictor, experiment CSVs, partition manifest, charts, and report were generated by this project's code. They are secondary analyses of the public annotations. Synthetic examples appear only inside automated tests; no synthetic data are used in the research results or dashboard.

## Independent live study v0.3.0

The independently collected study uses publicly labeled benchmark questions, not the SEVRA generated responses. Its immutable manifests and download checksums are in `outputs/live_study/v0.3.0/study_plan.json`. Question selection uses seed 42 and fixed pool seeds 42–45 before collecting model outcomes. The separate `trusted_keys.jsonl` supplies offline grading only; `questions.jsonl` contains no answers, worked solutions, correctness labels, or revised responses.

- **GSM8K, main configuration:** [OpenAI dataset card pinned at revision `740312add88f781978c0658806c59bc2815b9866`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/openai/gsm8k/blob/740312add88f781978c0658806c59bc2815b9866/README.md), [original repository](https://github.com/openai/grade-school-math), Cobbe et al. (2021), [Training Verifiers to Solve Math Word Problems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.14168). The publisher's card states MIT. Public numeric answer keys after the `####` delimiter make transparent offline numeric grading practical; this is the reason for choosing GSM8K rather than the public pilot's MATH source. The 600 training and 200 development questions come from original `train`; the 200 held-out maths questions come from original `test`. GSM8K has no published question ID column, so the pinned Parquet row index identifies each source question.
- **CommonsenseQA:** [Tel Aviv University dataset card pinned at revision `94630fe30dad47192a8546eb75f094926d47e155`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/tau/commonsense_qa/blob/94630fe30dad47192a8546eb75f094926d47e155/README.md), [original project](https://www.tau-nlp.org/commonsenseqa), Talmor, Herzig, Lourie and Berant (2019), [CommonsenseQA: A Question Answering Challenge Targeting Commonsense Knowledge](https://aclanthology.org/N19-1421/). The publisher's card states MIT. All five labeled options are included in each question; the separate key uses the published `answerKey`. The 200 adaptation questions come from original `train`; the 300 fixed target-test questions come from labeled `validation`. The official unlabeled test split is excluded. This held-out validation subset is the new study's evaluation set, not an official leaderboard submission. Published question IDs are retained.

The loader resolves full Hugging Face commit IDs once, downloads only commit-pinned files, verifies available repository LFS checksums, and records file SHA256 hashes. Local pinned cards and benchmark Parquet files are cached beneath the run's `raw/`; these bulk snapshots are excluded from the diagnostic bundle. Global normalization covers the full question, including choice options. Cross-original-split or cross-domain overlapping groups are excluded before selection; within-split duplicates keep one stable source record, and conflicting duplicate keys are rejected. The prepared manifest rejects duplicate groups and original-split leakage.

The planned 1,000 maths and 500 target questions are collection targets. They do not guarantee sufficient rare HURT outcomes or reliable model comparisons. The public SEVRA files and v0.1/v0.2 results remain separate and unchanged.
